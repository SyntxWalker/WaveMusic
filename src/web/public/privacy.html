DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

At WaveMusic, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. We are dedicated to providing a high-fidelity, transparent, and clutter-free Discord music bot with a strict data-minimization approach. This Privacy Policy details what information is processed, how it is utilized, and how your server’s privacy is protected.

2. Voice Audio Policy (Zero Recording Guarantee)

WaveMusic NEVER records, stores, processes, transcribes, monitors, or transmits user microphone audio or voice communications.

When WaveMusic joins a Discord voice channel, its audio engine operates exclusively in transmit/output mode to stream requested music. The bot does not tap into the incoming voice gateway streams, ensuring 100% voice privacy for every member in the channel.

3. Information We Collect & Store

We only store the minimal technical parameters necessary for bot functionality:

  • Guild (Server) Settings: Stored in a local SQLite database (wavemusic.db). This includes:
    • Discord Guild ID (to identify your server).
    • Custom Server Prefix (configured via /prefix, e.g. + or !).
    • 24/7 Voice Channel ID and Text Channel ID (to enable auto-reconnection when 24/7 mode is toggled on).
    • Default server playback volume level.
  • Temporary Interaction Data: When a user executes a command (e.g. /play), their Discord User ID and username are temporarily processed in memory to display "Requested by @User" on the Miniplayer card. This information is never stored permanently or sold.

4. Information We Never Collect

WaveMusic does NOT collect, request, or have access to:

  • Personal user messages, chat history, or direct messages (DMs).
  • Passwords, email addresses, or payment credentials.
  • IP addresses or hardware identifiers of Discord members.
  • Voice channel conversations or microphone feeds.

5. Third-Party Services & APIs

To deliver music playback, WaveMusic interacts with the following external services solely for real-time resolution:

  • Discord API: For bot gateway connectivity, command handling, and voice transmission.
  • Spotify Public Web API / Catalogue: For fetching official studio track metadata, artist names, and high-resolution album artwork.
  • YouTube Music / Topic: For matching and streaming licensed audio master recordings.
  • LRCLIB: For retrieving public, synchronized and plain song lyrics.

No user data is ever transmitted to advertisers, telemetry aggregators, or marketing brokers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Guild settings are retained only as long as WaveMusic remains an active member of your server. If you remove the bot from your Discord server, your settings become dormant.

Server administrators can request immediate and permanent deletion of all stored guild records at any time by contacting our support or by resetting guild configuration with the bot's reset tools.

7. Contact & Support

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your server’s data, please reach out via our official Discord support channels or repository.
